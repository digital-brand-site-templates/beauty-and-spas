Beautica — Beauty Salon Website Template
========================================

Version 2.0 — 2026
-------------------

- Rebuilt on Bootstrap 5 with every behaviour in plain JavaScript (js/main.js):
  the menu, sticky header, hero and testimonial carousels, photo hover swaps,
  search and back-to-top need no other library.
- Seven new pages: online booking (treatment, stylist, date and time, your
  details and a live summary), a price list with a search box and topic
  filters, a service detail page, products and gift cards with an enquiry
  form, a stylist profile, a privacy policy and a 404 page.
- Four ready-to-send HTML emails: booking confirmation, contact reply,
  newsletter welcome and product enquiry reply.
- The home page booking band is now a labelled booking panel; the hero
  slides carry a caption with Book now and Price list buttons; each service
  card shows its price and duration with Details and Book buttons.
- Every inner page opens with a photo banner, title, intro line and
  breadcrumb. About gains our story and a call to action; Services gains a
  treatment menu, visit steps and an FAQ; Gallery gains topic filters; the
  blog gains topics, a newsletter band, an author box and a redesigned
  comment section; Contact gains opening hours and a click-to-load map.
- The header search opens full screen on every device and lands on the
  price list with the results filtered.
- Every form has labels, validates and confirms; placeholders are readable
  on light forms.
- Real copy for a salon in place of the lorem-ipsum blocks, one address
  site-wide, working contact and social links, and a meta description on
  every page.
- A refreshed look that keeps the gold and navy identity: pill buttons,
  rounded cards with soft shadows, a sliding sticky header and a readable
  green service card (one css file, css/_modern.css, removable).
- Phone and tablet: the menu fits on one row on tablets, the footer shows
  two by two, grids show two columns on tablet, the four counters sit two
  by two on phones, and nothing scrolls sideways from 320px up.
- Each page now highlights its own item in the footer links.
- Fonts self-hosted; Font Awesome 4 -> 6.
