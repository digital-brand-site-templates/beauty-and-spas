Full body massage

Swedish or deep-tissue, 60 or 90 minutes — the treatment our clients rebook most.

  • Home
  • Services
  • Full body massage

what it is

A full-body massage that works from your feet to your scalp, with long flowing strokes to relax you and firmer work wherever you are holding tension — usually shoulders, neck and lower back. Choose Swedish for relaxation or deep-tissue if you train, sit at a desk all day or carry stress in your muscles. We use warm, unscented oils unless you ask for an aromatherapy blend.

what is included

  • A short consultation about pressure, problem areas and health
  • Back, neck, shoulders, arms, hands, legs and feet
  • A scalp massage to finish (90-minute treatment)
  • Herbal tea and ten quiet minutes in the lounge afterwards

before and after

What should I wear?

Whatever is comfortable — you undress to your level of comfort and are covered with towels throughout, except the area being massaged.

Can I have a massage if I am pregnant?

Yes, from the second trimester, with our pregnancy massage — tell us when you book so we can prepare the right table and pillows.

How often should I come?

For general relaxation, once a month. If you are working on a specific problem, every one to two weeks for the first month often helps most.
