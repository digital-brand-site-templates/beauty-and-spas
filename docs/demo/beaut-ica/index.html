about our salon

Beaut-ica opened on Old York Drive in 2018 with three chairs and one idea: that a salon visit should leave you feeling looked after, not rushed. Today our team of 21 stylists, therapists and make-up artists covers everything from a quick fringe trim to a full bridal morning — in a bright, calm space with the kettle always on.

Every visit starts with a proper consultation. We ask how you live, how much time you want to spend on your hair and skin at home, and what you have loved or hated before — then we recommend what will actually work for you. We use professional, cruelty-free products, and we are happy to tell you exactly what went on your hair or skin.
