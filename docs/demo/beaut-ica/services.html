good to know

Do I need a patch test?

Yes, for any new color, lash or brow tint, or skin peel — at least 48 hours before. It takes five minutes and it is free.

Can I change or cancel my appointment?

Free of charge up to 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours we may charge half of the treatment price, as that time was kept for you.

How early should I arrive?

Five to ten minutes before your time is perfect — enough to settle in and fill out a short consultation card on your first visit.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes — for any amount or any treatment, printed in the salon or sent by email. See Product & Gifts.

Is there parking?

Free parking for clients behind the salon, and a bus stop (routes 7 and 19) right outside.
