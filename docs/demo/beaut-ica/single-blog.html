Wedding hair: how to plan your trial

September 12th, 2026 · by Avery Lane · 5 min read

A hair trial is the single best thing you can do for your wedding morning. Book it six to eight weeks before the day — close enough that your hair length and color will be the same, far enough that there is time to change your mind. Bring photos of styles you love and one or two you do not, your veil or hair accessory, and a picture of your dress so we can see the neckline. Wear a top with a similar neckline if you can.

The trial is not only about the style — it is how you find out whether you feel like yourself in it. That is the thing you will see in every photo.

During the trial we try the style, take photos from every angle and then leave it alone for a few hours: the best test is how it looks after lunch, a walk and a hug. Tell us honestly what you think — we would much rather change it now than on the morning. We note every pin, product and heat setting, so the style on the day is the one you approved. Finally, plan your color or cut two to three weeks before the wedding, never the week of it.

Avery Lane
