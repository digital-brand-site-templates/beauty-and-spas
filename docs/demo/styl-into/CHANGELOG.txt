Stylinto — Fashion Store Ecommerce Website Template
===================================================

Version 2.0 — 2026
-------------------

- A completely new design, "Editorial Atelier": Fraunces headlines with
  Inter text on warm paper, ink and terracotta, arch-framed photos, a
  sticky frosted header with a category mega menu, a scrolling category
  strip and a terracotta newsletter band. Every page, header and footer
  was redesigned; all of the original pages, links and headings remain.
- Rebuilt on Bootstrap 5 with every behaviour in plain JavaScript
  (js/main.js): the category tree, product gallery, tabs, quantity
  steppers, checkout steps, footer folds on phones, back-to-top and
  every form. Font Awesome 4 -> 6; all fonts are self-hosted.
- Nine new pages: order confirmation, my account (orders, addresses,
  details), wishlist, about, contact (form, map, store hours), help &
  FAQ (delivery, returns, privacy, terms and a site map), size guide,
  forgot password and a 404 page.
- Six ready-to-send HTML emails, one per form: registration welcome,
  order confirmation, review received, contact auto-reply, password
  reset and newsletter welcome (email-*.html). They adapt to phones.
- Shop pages: product cards with a second photo on hover and a quick
  add-to-cart bar, a filter sidebar (a slide-out drawer on phones and
  tablets), sort and view toggles, pagination and product names in
  place of "Girls Top" on every card.
- Product page: a clickable photo gallery, size pills with a size-guide
  link, a quantity stepper, description and review tabs, a star-rating
  review form and related products.
- Cart and checkout: line items with quantity controls, a sticky order
  summary, coupon and gift-certificate panels, and six checkout steps
  that open one after another. The totals now add up.
- Every form is labelled, validates and confirms: login, register,
  forgot password, reviews, coupons, contact, account details and the
  newsletter. Real copy replaces the placeholder text.
- Phones and tablets: a burger menu with search and the category tree,
  two-column product grids on tablets and phones, and no sideways
  scrolling from 320px up.
- Fixes: 20 dead links (two of them reloaded the page), missing alt
  text and form labels, 60 inline styles, the keyboard focus outline
  that was switched off everywhere, and a shopping cart whose totals
  did not match its items.
