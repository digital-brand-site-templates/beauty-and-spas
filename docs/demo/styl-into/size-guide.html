How to measure

  1. Bust / chest Around the fullest part, keeping the tape level under your arms.
  2. Waist Around your natural waistline — the narrowest part of your torso.
  3. Hips Around the fullest part of your hips, feet together.
  4. Inside leg From the top of your inner thigh down to the floor.

Still unsure? Ask our style team — send your measurements and we'll suggest a size.
