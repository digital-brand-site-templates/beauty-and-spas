Hot Stone Massage

Hot stone massage

Smooth basalt stones, warmed to just above body temperature, are placed along the spine and worked over the muscles with long strokes. The heat lets the therapist go deeper without pressure, so it suits people who find a deep-tissue massage too much and a Swedish one too little.

What it includes

  • Ten-minute consultation on pressure, problem areas and any injuries
  • Sixty minutes on the table with warm stones and unscented oil
  • Fifteen minutes in the quiet lounge afterwards, with tea
  • A note of what was worked on, for next time

Before you come

Eat lightly, skip the coffee and arrive ten minutes early. Tell us about pregnancy, recent surgery, high blood pressure or skin conditions when you book — the stones are not for everyone and we will suggest a cool-stone or Swedish massage instead.
