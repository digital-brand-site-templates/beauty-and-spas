Spacelon — Beauty Salon & Spa HTML Template
============================================

Version 2.0 — 2026
------------------

A rebuild on a modern stack — the same design, checked block by block
against version 1 at desktop, tablet and phone widths — plus the pages the
site always pointed at but never had, an appointment-confirmation email in
the same design, and the things that were broken in version 1 quietly
fixed.


NEW
- appointment.html — the booking page behind "Make An Appointment" (the
  header button, the offer band, the four "Select Plan" buttons): service,
  date, time, name, phone, email, a note, with opening hours and the
  salon's phone and address beside the form.
- blog.html and single-blog.html — the listing and the post behind the
  home page's three blog cards, which had nothing to click in version 1.
  The post ends in a "Book This Treatment" button.
- 404.html — a not-found page with three ways back in.
- email-appointment.html — the confirmation a client receives after
  booking: the details in a box, add-to-calendar and change buttons,
  before-you-come notes, address and hours. Table-based, inline-styled,
  600px wide, with an Outlook conditional and a phone layout — the way
  mail clients need it. Before sending, replace the images/... paths with
  absolute URLs on your server and put your links in place of the "#" ones
  (the file says so at the top).

FIXED (things that were broken in version 1)
- No page had a main heading (h1). Each page's first block title — and
  the first slide's title on the home page — is the h1 now, same look.
- The header's envelope icon showed a PHONE number, linked as a phone
  number; it shows the salon's email address.
- The logo linked to #myPage, an anchor that exists on no page.
- 82 "#" links now go somewhere: both Make An Appointment buttons and the
  four Select Plan buttons to the booking page, the two slides' Read More
  to the services, the about blurb's to the about page, the sixteen expert
  social icons to the networks, the footer's six as well.
- The counters start when they scroll into view (they ran on page load,
  before the section was on screen).
- The newsletter field is an email field with a label and a name; the
  gallery lightbox opens with a keyboard and closes on Escape.
- The white feature icons on the gradient discs had turned black under
  Bootstrap 5; the gallery filter tabs had slid to the left; the about
  block's Read More had stretched to the full width. All as version 1.

DESIGN
- Unchanged. The fonts are version 1's own (Poppins, Dosis, Dancing
  Script), self-hosted as before — but only the five cuts the stylesheet
  uses ship (100 KB where version 1 packed 36 files, 1 MB).

UNDER THE HOOD
- Bootstrap 3.3.6 + jQuery 3.2 + counterup + waypoints + lightbox.js →
  Bootstrap 5.3, no jQuery. js/main.js is one dependency-free file: the
  lightbox, the counters, the menu closing after a tap, form validation,
  back-to-top. The carousel and the gallery tabs are Bootstrap's own.
- Font Awesome 4 → 6 (woff2 subset).
- css/bs3-compat.css: the Bootstrap 3 behaviours this design relies on
  (the 1170 container, panel metrics, form control heights) restored on top
  of Bootstrap 5. Safe to trim.
- Every page: a unique <title> and meta description, lang="en", one h1,
  no inline styles, no inline scripts, every image with alt text and
  width/height (no layout shift), lazy loading below the fold, labelled
  form fields, https links.
- home.html redirects to index.html (the entry page is index.html now).
