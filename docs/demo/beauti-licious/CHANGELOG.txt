Beautilicious — Spa and Beauty Salon HTML Template
==================================================

Version 2.0 — 2026
------------------

A rebuild on a modern stack, five pages the site needed but never had, a
blog with a working search, an appointment confirmation email, and a
modernised home page in the template's own colours: charcoal, white and
gold.


FIVE NEW PAGES AND AN EMAIL
- service-details.html — the page behind every service circle and tile:
  the treatment, what it includes, what to know before you come, a
  before-and-after pair, and a booking card with duration, price,
  therapist and room, "Book an appointment" and "See all prices".
- team-member.html — a profile behind every team photo: photo, role,
  facts (with us since, specialities, languages, hours), social links,
  the story, the treatments they do with prices, certifications, and
  the rest of the team.
- prices.html — the salon's price list: Hair, Face, Nails and Spa, each
  line with its duration and price and linked to the treatment, plus
  three packages (Half day, Full day, Bridal party).
- appointment.html — the booking form on its own page: treatment,
  stylist, date, time, name, phone, email, first visit, notes; validates
  and confirms; "What happens next" in three steps.
- 404.html — a not-found page in the template's design with four ways
  back in.
- email-appointment.html — the confirmation a client gets after booking:
  the logo bar and photograph, the booking card (treatment, with, date,
  time, price), three "before you come" steps, "Add to calendar" and
  "Change it". Table based and inline styled so it renders in Gmail,
  Outlook and Apple Mail; {{name}}, {{service}}, {{stylist}}, {{date}},
  {{time}}, {{price}} and {{email}} are the mailer's placeholders. A
  how-to-use note is at the top of the file.

THE HOME PAGE, MODERNISED
- Hero: a headline, a line and two buttons over each slide; below 992px
  the copy sits under the photograph as a charcoal band.
- Services: the four bare circles (the name only showed on hover) are
  cards on the charcoal band — photo, name in gold, a line and a
  "from $" price; the whole card is the link.
- "Impressed with us?": the Lorem line is real copy.
- Team: name and role under every photo (version 1 showed the name on
  hover only); the social icons still appear on hover.
- Testimonial: one centred quote — photo, words, name — instead of a
  photo column beside a justified block.
- Counters in gold; a gold focus ring on the form; a gold rule on top
  of the footer.
- Gold buttons use charcoal text (7:1) rather than white (2.2:1).

WORKING WHERE IT WAS DECORATIVE
- Search: both header boxes submit to blog.html?q=… and filter the
  posts; the tags on a post do the same.
- Blog: four posts (version 1 had two) so the search has something to
  filter.
- 76 bare "#" links in version 1 — the service names and circles, the
  team photos and names, Read More, Get an appointment, the tags, the
  popular posts, the social icons — every one goes somewhere.
- Forms validate and confirm: appointment (home, appointment page),
  contact, comments, search. Every field has a label, a name, a type and
  autocomplete. The Clear buttons are gone.
- The team carousel works (version 1's was broken: a 271px strip);
  Prev / Next and dots, no jQuery.
- The gallery's overlays work on touch and keyboard (:focus-within), and
  the photographs open in a lightbox.
- The footer's Instagram strip shows six photographs; version 1 drew it
  at runtime from a dead Instagram API with an access token shipped in
  the page. The token is gone.
- Counters animate on scroll; the off-canvas menu opens, closes on Esc
  and outside click, and reports its state to screen readers.

CONTENT
- 58 Lorem Ipsum passages are salon copy of the same length, so every
  block keeps its height.
- A title and meta description per page; alt text on every image.

UNDER THE HOOD
- Bootstrap 3.3.6 + jQuery → Bootstrap 5.3, no jQuery, one
  dependency-free js/main.js (owl replacement, off-canvas, lightbox,
  counters, search, forms, back to top).
- Font Awesome 4 (1.1 MB of eot / ttf / svg / otf) → Font Awesome 6 as
  three woff2 files (304 KB). Aileron: sixteen Font Squirrel families
  (813 KB) → one "Aileron" family in six woff2 cuts (124 KB) with real
  weights, so font-weight works as CSS expects. antonio-exotic (never
  used) dropped. Nothing is loaded from a third party.
- magnific-popup, lightbox, owl.carousel, classie, modernizr,
  counterup, waypoints, inview, instafeed, scrolling-nav dropped: main.js
  covers what the design used.
- css/bs3-compat.css: the Bootstrap 3 behaviours this design relies on
  (15px base type, fixed heading sizes, the 1170 container, the navbar's
  layout) restored on top of Bootstrap 5. Safe to trim.
- style.css section 8 holds the modernised look; delete it to get the
  version 1 home page back on the same markup.
- The entry page is index.html (version 1: home.html, kept as a redirect).
- No inline styles or inline event handlers, no duplicate ids, lang="en",
  https links.
