january 13th, 2017

We are a full-service salon and day spa with twelve stylists and therapists, open seven days a week. Every visit starts with a consultation: what you want, what your hair or skin needs, and how long it takes. Our colourists train every season, and every colour we use is ammonia-free and tested on nobody. The spa has four treatment rooms, a steam room and a quiet lounge with tea and no phones. Two minutes from the station.
