Our story

Clothes worth keeping.

Stylinto began as a single rail of hand-picked knitwear in a New York market stall. Today we dress women, men and children in 38 countries — and we still choose every piece as if it were for that first rail.

2012since

Made to be worn for years, not weeks

We design in small runs, so nothing is made that nobody wants. Our knitwear comes from two family mills we have worked with since the start, and our denim is cut from organic cotton in a factory that runs on solar power.

Every style is tested for a season by our own team before it reaches the shop floor. If it pills, stretches or fades too fast, it doesn't make the cut.
