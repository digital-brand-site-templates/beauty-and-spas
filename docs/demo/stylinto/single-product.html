Cut from a fine, brushed knit with a little stretch, this top has a wide fold-over neckline you can wear on or off the shoulders. The body is relaxed and finishes just above the hip, so it sits neatly over high-waisted jeans or tucks into a midi skirt.

Long raglan sleeves end in a soft rib cuff. It is garment-dyed after knitting, which gives the colour its lived-in depth — and means it won't shrink when you wash it at 30°.

  • Fabric 70% viscose, 25% nylon, 5% elastane
  • Fit Relaxed — take your usual size
  • Length 58 cm (size M)
  • Care Machine wash 30°, dry flat
