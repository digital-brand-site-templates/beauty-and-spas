Delivery Information

How much does delivery cost?

Standard delivery is free on orders over $75 and $5 below that. Express next-day delivery is $9 when you order before 2pm.

How long will my order take?

Standard delivery takes 3–5 working days. International orders take 5–10 working days depending on the destination.

Can I track my parcel?

Yes — we email a tracking link the moment your order leaves our warehouse, and you can follow it from your account.

Returns

What is your returns policy?

You have 30 days from delivery to return anything unworn with its tags on. Returns are free within the U.S.A, U.K and India.

How do I start a return?

Go to Order History, choose the items and print the prepaid label — or book a free collection from your door.

When will I get my refund?

Within five working days of the parcel reaching us, to the original payment method.

Sizing & Products

How do I find my size?

Every product lists its fit, and our size guide shows exact measurements for women, men and children.

How should I care for knitwear?

Wash at 30° inside out, and dry flat. Most of our knits are garment-dyed, so they won't shrink or fade quickly.

Privacy Policy

What data do you keep?

Only what we need to deliver your order and run your account: your name, contact details, addresses and order history. We never sell your data.

How do I delete my account?

Email hello@stylinto.com from the address on your account and we'll remove your data within 30 days.

Terms & Conditions

Prices and payment

Prices include VAT where it applies. We accept Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, Apple Pay and cash on delivery in selected regions.

Pre-orders

Pre-order items are charged when they ship. You can cancel a pre-order at any time before then at no cost.
