About Time-center

A small house for good watches

We are watchmakers first and a shop second. Every piece we sell has been on our bench before it reaches your wrist.

Started at one bench, in 2017

Time-center began as a repair counter on Clockmaker's Row. Customers kept asking where to buy a watch they could trust, so we started keeping a few pieces we had serviced ourselves.

Today we stock around two hundred watches from eight independent houses. The counter is now a workshop with four benches, and every watch still passes through one of them before it is boxed. We open the case back, check the movement, regulate it, pressure-test the seals and fit the strap by hand.

It takes longer than shipping straight from a warehouse. It also means that the watch you open keeps time from the first day, and that if it ever needs us, the people who checked it are the people who answer the phone.

What every watch goes through

Four checks, one bench, about ninety minutes a watch.

  1. Inspected

    Case, crystal, dial and hands checked under a loupe for marks, dust and alignment.

  2. Regulated

    Automatic and hand-wound movements timed in six positions and adjusted to within seconds a day.

  3. Sealed

    Gaskets checked and every case pressure-tested to its rated depth before the back goes on.

  4. Boxed by hand

    Strap fitted and sized, warranty card signed by the watchmaker who did the work.
