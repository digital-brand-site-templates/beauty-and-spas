The Halden 38 is the watch our workshop reaches for first: a plain, honest dial that reads at a glance, and an automatic movement that asks nothing more than to be worn.

The 38 mm case is brushed on top and polished on the flanks, so it catches the light without shouting. A domed sapphire crystal sits over a silvered dial with applied indices and a red-tipped seconds hand, the one flash of colour in an otherwise quiet face.

Inside is a Swiss automatic calibre with a 42-hour power reserve, regulated in our workshop to within six seconds a day before it leaves. The case back is screwed down for 50 metres of water resistance, and the brown calfskin strap is fitted with a quick-release pin so you can change it in seconds.
