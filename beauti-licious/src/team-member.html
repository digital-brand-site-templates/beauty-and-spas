About David

David trained in London and has cut and coloured hair for fourteen years, the last eleven of them here. He runs our colour line — every shade we use was chosen on his chair — and in 2021 qualified as a massage therapist so he could take on the spa's hot-stone sessions, which are now the most-booked treatment in the building.

Clients come back to David for the consultation as much as the cut: he will tell you what will and will not work with your hair, and why, before anything is touched.

Treatments

  • Cutting and styling — 45 minutes, from $55
  • Colour and highlights — 90 to 150 minutes, from $120
  • Hot stone massage — 75 minutes, $95
  • Bridal hair — trial and day, from $220

Certifications

  • Level 3 Diploma in Hairdressing, London College of Beauty Therapy
  • Wella Master Colour Expert
  • ITEC Diploma in Holistic Massage, hot stone endorsement

Say hello

Ask for David by name when you book, or leave a note in the appointment form and we will match you up. He keeps a record of every colour formula so a repeat visit picks up exactly where the last one left off.
