Customer care

The short answers. For anything else, send us a message above.

Delivery information

Every order ships insured and signed-for. Metro cities in 2–4 working days, the rest of India in 3–6, and international orders in 5–9. Express next-day delivery is available at checkout for $18.

Returns

Changed your mind? Return an unworn watch with its tags and box within 30 days for a full refund. We send a prepaid, insured courier to collect it.

Privacy policy

We collect only what we need to deliver and service your watch, never sell your details, and delete account data on request. Payments are handled by our card processor; we never see your full card number.

Terms & conditions

Prices include tax. Pre-order pieces are charged when they ship. The two-year warranty covers the movement and manufacturing faults; it does not cover straps, glass damage or water entry after the crown was left open.

Gift vouchers

Vouchers from $50 to $1,000, sent by email or in a printed card with your message. Valid for three years against any watch, strap or service.

Affiliates

Write about watches? Our affiliate programme pays 6% on referred orders, with a 30-day cookie. Email us with a link to your site.
