about our salon

Beautica opened on Old York Drive in 2018 with three chairs, one treatment room and a simple idea: that a salon visit should leave you feeling looked after, not rushed. Word spread, the team grew, and today 21 stylists, therapists and make-up artists work across two floors — still with the kettle on and the same unhurried welcome.

We are an independent salon, owned and run by the people who work in it. That means we choose our products because they work, not because a distributor asked us to; we train every month; and every client gets a proper consultation before anything touches their hair or skin. It also means we know most of our clients by name.

our advantages

Prices are on our price list before you book — no surprises at the till — and if anything is not right within a week, we put it right for free.

  • trained every month

    Every stylist and therapist is qualified and trains with us monthly — current techniques, not habits.

  • unhurried appointments

    We book generous times, so your consultation, treatment and finish are never rushed.

  • patch-tested first

    A free patch or strand test before every new color, lash or skin treatment.

  • cruelty-free products

    Professional, vegan-friendly brands we would happily use on ourselves.
