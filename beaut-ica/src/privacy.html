Privacy policy

Last updated 27 September 2026 — what we do with the details you send us.

  • Home
  • Privacy policy

who we are

Beaut-ica Salon & Spa (“Beaut-ica”, “we”), 701 Old York Drive, Richmond, VA 23225. This policy explains what we do with the information you give us through this website. Questions go to admin@example.com.

what we collect

Only what you type into our forms: your name, email address and phone number, and anything you add in a message — through booking, the contact form, a product or gift card enquiry, a blog comment or the newsletter. For a booking we also keep the treatment, stylist, date and time you chose, and any notes such as allergies you tell us about.

how we use it

To confirm and run your appointment, answer your message, and — only if you signed up — send the newsletter. Allergy and skin notes are seen only by the stylist or therapist treating you. We never sell your information or share it for marketing.

cookies

This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies. The map on our contact page loads only if you press “Show the map”; Google Maps then sets its own cookies under Google’s privacy policy.

how long we keep it

Booking and consultation records: three years after your last visit, then deleted. Messages and enquiries: one year. Newsletter: until you unsubscribe — every email has a one-click link.

your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time and at no cost. Write to admin@example.com and we will reply within 30 days.
